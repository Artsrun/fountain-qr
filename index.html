DECIMEN NOTES Open demo

Fountain codes over a camera

Screen → light → QR → peel. No Wi-Fi path for the file itself.

1. What the clip claimed

Two phones. Red TX paints QR v40 at 60 fps, 2953 B/frame. Blue RX camera locks and peels. Result: 365 KB in 2.6 s (140.42 KB/s). Payload was Rick Astley. Stream never ends; RX stops on hash.

The snow is not analog noise. It is dense QR modules changing faster than the eye tracks.

Hygiene: payload travels as light. Both pages still loaded from a URL (decimen.dfs.lan). Not encrypted. Any camera pointed at the glass can reconstruct.

OCC bottleneck = camera fps × bytes/frame. LiFi bottleneck = LED/PD bandwidth. Pick OCC for two phones now. Pick LiFi for an RF-denied room with ceiling kits.

3. Fountain theory, one example

File HELP, one letter per block, K = 4.

x1=H  x2=E  x3=L  x4=P

Each droplet: pick d block IDs, XOR those blocks, ship XOR + the ID list.

seqIDssent
0–3{1} {2} {3} {4}raw H E L P (systematic)
4{1,3}H⊕L
5{2,4}E⊕P

Camera drops E. Received: H, L, P, H⊕L, E⊕P.

Peel: degree-1 droplets give H, L, P. Then

x2 = (E⊕P) ⊕ P = E

Never received E as its own frame. That is the decoder.

Need about K × (1 + ε) distinct droplets. Decimen quotes ε ≈ 0.15. Duplicates and unreadable QRs are erasures — ignore them. No ACK.

4. Degree is the knob

  • d = 1 starts the peel (ripple).
  • d = 2 turns one known block into another.
  • d ≈ K is a smoothie of everything — useless until you already have K−1.

Ideal soliton keeps the ripple at ~1 — one blur kills it. Robust soliton over-weights d=1 so the ripple stays alive. This demo uses a toy table (systematic + d∈{1,2,3}) that is enough for small K.

6. Demo protocol

DCI1 | session | seq | K | blockSize | fileLen | name | idxs | XOR payload

First K frames are raw blocks. Then mixed droplets. jsQR miss = erasure. peel() while a droplet has one unknown index. Stop when recovered === K.

Phone-camera safe defaults: 48 B blocks, 12 fps, ECC M. Not Decimen v40 density.
