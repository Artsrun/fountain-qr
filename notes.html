Why v2 is not Decimen

Decimen uses QR v40 @ ~60 fps, a zxing-cpp WASM decoder, and 2–4 tiled codes. That is how they post 200–400 KB/s receipts. This project uses jsQR in the page so a phone camera locks. Density is a preset, not the default.

Wire is DCI2 (gzip flag). Not D1C3.
